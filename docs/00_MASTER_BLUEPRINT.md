## 1. Mission & Vision
Build a unified, modular, open-standards-based defensive cybersecurity ecosystem helping authorized Government agencies, Banking & Financial Institutions, Enterprises, Technology Companies, and Research Institutions assess security posture, prioritize risks, detect threats, manage incidents, preserve cryptographic digital evidence, secure AI systems, and integrate verified security tools under strict governance.

## 2. NIST CSF 2.0 Core Alignment
- **Govern:** Policies, accountability, approvals, risk governance
- **Identify:** Hardware/software/cloud/API/AI asset inventory & risk scoring
- **Protect:** MFA, RBAC, Tenant Isolation, Encryption, Secure Configuration
- **Detect:** Security event ingestion, alert correlation, anomaly monitoring
- **Respond:** Incident investigation, containment, human-approved workflows
- **Recover:** Verified backups, disaster recovery, business continuity
